About us

We make things that work better and last longer.

Our story

Fewer, better things.

We started with a simple frustration: the hoodie you love most is always the one that wears out first. So we set out to make the pieces you reach for every day — and make them properly.

That means heavyweight fabrics, generous cuts and construction that holds up to real life. It means sneakers designed to be worn, not kept in a box. And it means saying no to anything that doesn’t earn its place.

Every collection is small on purpose. We would rather get ten things right than a hundred things almost right.
